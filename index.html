Addition

  • Level 1: Generates numbers within the range of 1-10 to add.
  • Level 2: Generates numbers within the range of 10 to 100 to add.
  • Level 3: Generates numbers with decimal places to add. Please make sure to round to the nearest 10th!

    • Subtraction

  • Level 1: Generates numbers within the range of 1-10 to subtract.
  • Level 2: Generates numbers within the range of 10 to 100 to subtract.
  • Level 3: Generates numbers with decimal places to subtract. Please make sure to round to the nearest 10th!

    • Multiplication

  • Level 1: Generates numbers within the range of 1-10 to multiply.
  • Level 2: Generates numbers within the range of 2 to 20 to multiply.
  • Level 3: Generates numbers with decimal places to multiply. Please make sure to round to the nearest 10th!

    • Division

  • Level 1: Generates numbers within the range of 2-20 to divide.
  • Level 2: Generates numbers within the range of 101 to 200 to divide.
  • Level 3: Generates numbers with decimal places to divide. Please make sure to round to the nearest 10th!

    • How to Use Features

  • Click the operation of your choice from the navigation bar on the top
  • Select the difficulty level and press the Start button to generate 10 questions on that operation
  • You will be presented with notifications when you get an incorrect answer
  • It may let you know if you incorrectly type in a whole number or decimal
  • When finished with the 10 questions, you will see a pop-up presenting your results
  • Click the sidebar on the right to get a report on your accuracy and timing anytime
  • The sidebar will show up to 5 rounds of your performance
